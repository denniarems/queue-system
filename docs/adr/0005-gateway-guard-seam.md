# Gateway Guard as the Single Seam to a Payment Gateway

We need one place that decides whether a call to a Payment Gateway may proceed, so that the Rate Limiter and the Circuit Breaker stop being sequenced by hand inside the Payment Saga. We decided to introduce a Gateway Guard: the Payment Saga calls `call(payment)` and `refund(...)`, and behind that seam the Guard waits for a Token Bucket token, consults the Circuit Breaker, invokes the Payment Gateway, classifies the outcome from `code` and `httpStatus`, and feeds the result back to both controls. Two consequences are part of the decision rather than accidents of the code. Compensation is exempt from that gating: a Circuit Breaker is usually OPEN precisely when a refund is needed, so gating compensation would leave a Payment charged, unsettled and unrefunded during the outage that made the refund necessary. And the domain owns `retryable`, derived from `code` through `RETRYABLE_CODES`, so the Payment Gateway adapter reports facts about what the provider said rather than policy about what we do with it.
